# Northwood College Student Help Facts
(Fictional college made up for this demo. Every fact the bot is allowed to use is in this file.)

## Library (Harper Library)
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- Sunday: 12:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- During exam weeks (December and April) the library is open until 2:00 a.m. on weekdays.
- Study rooms can be booked up to 7 days ahead at library.northwood.example/rooms. Maximum 3 hours per booking.

## Fees and deadlines
- Winter term tuition is due January 15.
- Fall term tuition is due September 10.
- Late payments get a $75 late fee.
- Questions about fees go to the Student Accounts Office in Room A-104, open weekdays 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

## Wellness and support
- The Wellness Centre offers free, confidential counselling to all students.
- Book counselling by calling 555-0142 or visiting Room W-210.
- After hours, students can call the 24/7 support line at 555-0199.
- Accessibility Services (Room W-220) arranges exam accommodations. Students should register at least 3 weeks before exams.

## Programs
- Northwood offers diplomas in Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Nursing, Business Administration, and Graphic Design.
- All programs are open to every student who meets the academic requirements. Admission does not depend on gender, background, or age.
- Program advisors can be booked through the Advising Office in Room B-115.

## Scholarships
- Northwood Entrance Award: $1,500 for students with an admission average of 85% or higher. Open to all.
- Community Leadership Award: $1,000 for students with strong volunteer records. Open to all.
- Women in STEM Award: $2,000 for women enrolled in Software or Electrical Engineering.
- Applications for all awards close March 1. Apply at awards.northwood.example.

## Clubs
- Robotics Club, AI Club, Coding Club, Women in Tech Circle, Debate Club, Photography Club, and Gaming Club.
- Clubs Fair is held in the second week of September in the Main Atrium.

## Campus services
- Campus Wi-Fi network: NW-Secure. Log in with your student number and portal password.
- IT Help Desk: Room A-020, or email ithelp@northwood.example.
- Student parking permits cost $220 per term and are sold at the Security Office (Room C-001).
- Front Desk (general questions): Main Building lobby, 555-0100.
